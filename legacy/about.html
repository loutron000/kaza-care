ABOUT KAZA-CARE

Supporting the people who keep our flocks thriving.

Poultry farming supports livelihoods and food supply across Nigeria. Kaza-Care aims to make poultry health information, record-keeping, and access to veterinary support easier for smallholder farmers.

Get started

THE CHALLENGE

When flock health is at risk, every decision matters.

Recognising signs of illness, keeping track of vaccinations, and knowing where to seek professional help can be difficult, especially when information and support are not easy to access.

01

Recognise concerns early

Changes in feeding, movement, egg production, or bird behaviour can signal a health concern. Knowing what to observe is an important first step.

02

Keep better records

Scattered notes and forgotten dates make it harder to follow flock health, remember vaccination schedules, and review previous concerns.

03

Find the right support

When birds become ill, farmers need practical information and a clear path towards appropriate veterinary advice.

OUR MISSION

Healthier Flocks. Stronger Farms.

Kaza-Care is designed to help smallholder poultry farmers make more informed flock-health decisions through accessible guidance, organised health records, vaccination tracking, and veterinary support resources.

Our starting focus is Plateau State, Nigeria, with an approach built around the practical needs of farmers and the importance of professional animal healthcare.

HOW IT HELPS

Practical tools for everyday poultry care.

Kaza-Care brings important flock-health tasks together in one place.

⌕

Health assessments

Organise observed symptoms and receive preliminary guidance on possible next steps. An assessment is not a veterinary diagnosis.

⌖

Veterinary support

Help farmers explore veterinary support options and understand when professional assessment is important.

▤

Health records

Keep flock observations, health concerns, and follow-up notes organised for easier reference.

✓

Vaccination tracking

Record vaccinations and track planned dates to help keep flock-health activities organised.

WHAT GUIDES US

Built around responsible poultry care.

  • Accessible by design Useful information should be straightforward to understand.
  • Guidance, not a verdict Digital tools can support decisions, but they do not replace qualified veterinary care.
  • Grounded in farmers' needs Tools should help with real tasks, from observing symptoms to maintaining records.
  • Honest and responsible Health information and service listings should be presented clearly, without overstating what is verified.
