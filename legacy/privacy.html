YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Kaza-Care may handle, how it is intended to be used, and the choices available to you when using the platform.

Last updated: 22 October 2026

On this page

Information we handle How information is used Farm and health records Location information Sharing information Storage and security Your choices Children's privacy Policy updates Contact us

1. Information we handle

Depending on the features used, Kaza-Care may handle information such as:

  • Account details, such as a name and email address.
  • Farm information, including flock size and bird type.
  • Poultry health observations, reported symptoms, vaccination entries, and related notes.
  • Location information when it is provided or a location-based feature is used.
  • Information you send when contacting the Kaza-Care team.

The information actually collected depends on the version of Kaza-Care in use and the features available at that time.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to support the platform's intended functions, including:

  • Helping farmers organise poultry health records.
  • Supporting vaccination tracking and follow-up reminders.
  • Providing preliminary information about reported symptoms.
  • Helping users explore veterinary support options.
  • Responding to questions and improving the user experience.

Kaza-Care is intended to support informed decisions, not replace a qualified veterinary professional's assessment.

3. Farm and poultry health records

Farm records and reported symptoms can reveal information about your poultry operations. Only enter information you are comfortable providing through the version of the platform you are using.

Keep your own copies of important farm records. Do not rely on an unconfirmed reminder or an experimental feature as your only record of a vaccination or veterinary instruction.

4. Location information

Location can help users explore veterinary support options relevant to their area. Where a location feature is available, review the information you provide and any location permissions requested by your browser.

Do not assume that every listed practitioner has been verified or is currently available. Confirm professional credentials and service details independently before relying on a listing.

5. Sharing information

Personal information should only be shared where necessary for an explained purpose, with appropriate permission or another applicable lawful basis.

If Kaza-Care introduces external service providers, integrations, or professional referral features, the relevant information handling arrangements should be explained before those features are used.

Do not submit passwords, banking credentials, or other sensitive information through general feedback or support messages.

6. Storage and security

The way information is stored depends on the technical version of Kaza-Care being used. Prototype features may use temporary or browser-local data and may not retain entries after a page is refreshed or closed.

No online service can promise absolute security. Before entering real personal or farm information, check which features are operational and how their data is handled.

7. Your choices

You should be able to make informed choices about the information you provide.

  • Provide only the information needed to use a feature.
  • Review browser location permissions where relevant.
  • Keep independent copies of important health and farm records.
  • Contact the team with questions about access, correction, or deletion of information, where applicable.

Available controls depend on the version of the platform. Clearing browser data may remove information stored locally.

8. Children's privacy

Kaza-Care is designed for people managing poultry farming activities, not specifically for children. Anyone using the platform should provide information only where they are authorised to do so.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Kaza-Care's features and information practices develop. The revision date at the top of this page indicates when this text was last updated.

The policy should be reviewed whenever new data collection, external integrations, or account features are introduced.

10. Contact us

If you have a question about this policy or how information is handled, contact the Kaza-Care team.

Email: kazacare@gmail.com

This page describes intended privacy principles for the platform. It is not a substitute for reviewing the actual data flows, security arrangements, and applicable legal requirements before a public production launch.
